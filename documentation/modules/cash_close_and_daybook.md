# Module: cash close and daybook
Status: Shift rules (P2-6a), shifts, blind close and daybook (P2-6b) and the close screens (P2-6c) built 2026-11-21; nobody has seen the screens. The daybook has no screen yet (P2-7 reports)

Purpose: end-of-shift cash count and the money ledger.

- **Asked by owner:** an accurate daily cash close and daily analytics.
- **Rules:** SSOT 17, 20; guardrail 2; ADR-0006.
- **Workflow:** cashier enters denominations, the system stores expected and declared cash, locks the shift and reports the variance to the owner only.
- **Owner decisions (2026-11-21):** the cashier opens a shift with the cash in the drawer (counted by notes and coins) and ends it with a blind count; sales and returns need an open shift; expected cash is opening cash plus cash kept from sales minus cash refunds (UPI, card and credit note money is not in the drawer); any difference is shown to the owner, no tolerance; the count lists notes 500, 200, 100, 50, 20, 10 and coins 10, 5, 2, 1.
- **Rules built (P2-6a):** `Denomination` (a 10 rupee note and coin are separate), `countCash` (a count over 100,000 of one denomination is taken as a typing mistake: assistant's choice), `expectedCash`, `CashVariance` (counted minus expected; negative is short), `ShiftCloseReceipt` (what the cashier gets back: no expected amount, no variance).
- **Plan:** P2-6b tables (`shifts`, `shift_closes`, `daybook`, shift id on bills and returns; schema v6; a shift row and its close row are never edited), P2-6c screens (the owner's shift list is the only place a variance shows; the daybook and reports are P2-7). Assistant's choices, unconfirmed: one open shift per cashier; a manager or owner who sells or takes a return also needs their own open shift; the daybook is a table written in the same transaction as the sale or return, one row per payment mode (not derived later).
- **Built in P2-6b (schema v6):** `ShiftRepository` (`apps/pos_windows/lib/data/shift_repository.dart`): `openShift` (drawer cash counted by notes and coins; one open shift per cashier), `currentShift`, `hasStaleShift` (opened on an earlier India day), `closeShift` (blind: returns only a `ShiftCloseReceipt`; stores expected cash and the variance in `shift_closes` for the owner), `closeReports` (needs the daybook permission: manager and owner only). Every sale and return needs the signed-in person's open shift (`NoOpenShift` otherwise; nothing is saved) and is stamped with it. The `daybook` table gets one row per payment mode of each bill (positive, cash net of change) and one negative row per refund (`SALE_CASH`, `SALE_UPI`, `SALE_CARD`, `SALE_CREDIT_NOTE`, `REFUND_*`), written in the same transaction; a zero amount is refused and no row is written for a free bill. Expected cash is read from the daybook (opening plus cash sales minus cash refunds). `ShiftGate` shows the open-shift form (`cash_count_form.dart`) in front of the register and the returns screen, for cashiers and for a manager or owner opening Billing or Returns. Owner choices (2026-11-21): a shift left open from an earlier day must be closed (blind) before selling again; the close is done from the register screen with no bill in progress; the opening count is only recorded, not compared with the last close. 
- **Close screens built (P2-6c):** a Close shift button on the register screen (`close_shift_screen.dart`), enabled only when no bill is in progress; the cashier counts by notes and coins, confirms in a dialog, sees "Shift closed" and a Lock the counter button. No screen of the close shows the expected amount, the variance, or anything but what was typed (a test reads every text on each step). A shift opened on an earlier India day must be closed the same way at sign-in before a new one can be opened (`ShiftGate`; the cashier sees no expected amount there either). The owner's list (`shift_closes_screen.dart`, Manager Mode button Shift closes) shows each close: cashier, opened and closed times, opening, expected, counted and Short, Over or Exact; a cashier who reaches the screen anyway is refused by the data layer. Not built: a daybook screen and daily reports (P2-7); a close of a shift by the owner on behalf of a cashier; a stale-shift check for someone who stays signed in past midnight (the idle lock signs them out after 5 minutes, and the next sign-in forces the close).
- **Tasks:** P2-6a to P2-6c, P2-7.
- **Evals first:** E-SHIFT-1, E-AUDIT-1.
- **Not in scope:** supplier payouts, expenses beyond petty cash (decide later).
- **Open decisions:** none.
