# Module: GST toggle
Status: first step built, GST off (2026-11-23); reports not built; every rule unverified

Purpose: the one-click path to GST invoices and reports when the shop registers. **All GST rules are unverified; the CA confirms them.**

- **Asked by owner:** a toggle; the owner enters GST details, chooses inclusive or exclusive billing, the rate (5% today, editable) and per-item or per-bill rounding.
- **Rules:** SSOT 12-14; ADR-0007.
- **Workflow:** release 1 stores tax fields on every sale item and keeps `gst_enabled` false. When enabled later, a setup screen collects the values and new bills use them; old bills are unchanged.
- **Tasks:** P3-4 (scaffold: settings, tax fields, field toggles). The catalogue below grows as the CA asks for more.
- **Evals first:** E-GST-1; a toggled-off field never prints, a toggled-on field always prints.
- **Not in scope:** e-invoicing, GST filing integrations.
- **Open decisions:** none. Which fields are legally mandatory is unverified; the CA confirms.

## Invoice field catalogue (owner toggles each; all unverified until the CA confirms)
Shop name; shop address; shop GSTIN; invoice number (unbroken series); invoice date and time; customer name; customer phone; customer GSTIN (for business buyers); item description; HSN code; quantity; unit price; discount; taxable value; GST rate; tax amount per item; CGST and SGST split; IGST; total tax; round-off; invoice total; amount in words; payment mode; cashier name; terms or return policy line; signature line.

## GST report catalogue (owner toggles each; all unverified until the CA confirms)
Sales summary by day and month; tax collected by rate; sales by HSN code; invoice register (list of invoices in a date range); returns and credit-note register; sales to business customers with GSTIN; purchases and input tax (needs purchase GST details, later); export to a spreadsheet file for the CA.

## Built 2026-11-23 (P3-4, first step; owner chose: setup, tax on bills and the printed invoice, owner only)
**GST is off until the owner switches it on, and every rule below is unverified until the CA confirms it (SSOT rule 14, E-GST-1).**
- **Who:** only the owner may switch GST on or change its settings (`configureGst` is now owner-only; ADR-0003 amended). Everyone at the counter can read the settings to show the total.
- **Where:** Manager Mode, Settings, a **GST** card (amber note: unverified, ask the CA). A manager sees a read-only line. The card holds: the switch, the shop GSTIN, the shop address, the rate (5% to start, any rate above 0 and up to 50%), "The price on the tag" (includes tax, or tax is added), "Tax is worked out" (on each garment, or once on the bill) and a chip for each invoice field (the first set below). Switching on needs a GSTIN shaped like `27ABCDE1234F1Z5` (the check character is **not** verified), an address and a rate; switched off, nothing has to be filled in and the choices are kept.
- **Data:** the settings are rows in `app_settings` (`gst_enabled`, `gst_mode`, `gst_rate_bp`, `gst_rounding`, `gstin`, `shop_address`, `gst_field_*`); a missing row means the default (GST off). Schema v12 adds to every bill line `gst_bp`, `taxable_paise`, `tax_paise` and `extra_tax_paise` (all 0 while GST is off and for every bill made before v12); bill lines never change, so a changed rate, a changed mode or switching GST off never touches a bill already made (ADR-0007 points 3, 5, 7).
- **Rules** (`packages/core_domain/lib/src/gst_rules.dart`): `billTax` works out each garment's tax from the price after markdown and discount. Tax included: taxable = price / (1 + rate) and tax = price - taxable. Tax added: tax = price x rate, added to what the customer pays. Fractions of a paisa round half up (ADR-0007 point 9). On each garment the tax rounds per garment; once on the bill it is worked out on the total and shared over the garments in whole paise (the largest fractions take the leftover paise). CGST is half the tax rounded up, SGST the rest (an intra-state sale only: IGST is not built). `amountInWords` writes the total the Indian way. Assistant's reading, to confirm with the CA: all of the above, the 50% typo guard, the GSTIN shape.
- **At the register:** the total includes the tax when it is added on top; a line under the total says "Includes GST ₹X" (and "added to the prices" when tax is added). The bill is priced again inside the database transaction from the stored settings, so the screen cannot set its own tax. If the owner changes GST while a bill is open, Complete bill stops once with the new total.
- **The printed bill:** with GST on it is headed TAX INVOICE and prints only the fields the owner switched on. First set (all can be switched): shop address, shop GSTIN, customer name, customer phone, MRP of each garment, markdown and discount, taxable value, GST rate, tax on each garment, CGST and SGST split, total tax, total in words, how it was paid, operator ID, a terms line (returns within the return window) and a signature line. The invoice number is the bill number (an unbroken series per counter and financial year). Fields in the full catalogue above that are **not** built yet: HSN code (needs a code on each style), customer GSTIN, round-off, IGST.
- **Returns and reports:** a return refunds exactly what was paid, tax included (`final_paise + extra_tax_paise`); the tax in a returned garment is taken back. The daily report adds "GST in these sales", "GST in garments returned" and "GST to pass on" when GST was used, and **profit leaves the tax out** (sales less tax less cost). Sales and net sales in the report still include the tax. Analytics use bill totals, so with tax added on top they include the tax (known; GST reports will separate it).
- **Not built (later steps):** the rest of the GST report catalogue (sales by HSN, sales to business customers; see "GST reports built" for what exists), per-style rates and HSN codes, customer GSTIN for business buyers, IGST, a credit note carrying tax.

## GST reports built (P3-4 second step, 2026-11-23)
**Unverified until the CA says which reports and columns they need.** Manager Mode, **GST reports** (manager and owner, `viewReports`): a range of days (From and To typed as day/month/year; chips Today, This month, Last month; this month to date at first).
- **Shows:** tiles (GST invoices, taxable value, tax charged, tax returned, net tax), how many bills in the range were made with GST off (counted, not listed), **tax by rate** (sales less returns), **day by day**, the **invoice register** (invoice number, date, customer name, taxable value, CGST, SGST, total) and the **returns register** (date, the invoices the garments were sold on, taxable value, CGST, SGST, refund).
- **Export for the CA:** **Export for the CA** asks for a folder and writes three CSV files, `gst-invoices-<from>-to-<to>.csv`, `gst-returns-...csv` and `gst-summary-...csv` (rate block, a blank line, day block). UTF-8 with a byte order mark so a spreadsheet reads names correctly; money as plain rupees (`1499.00`); a field that could run as a formula (starting with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@`) is defused with an apostrophe.
- **From what is stored:** only the tax kept on each bill line (so a later change of rate or mode never alters a past figure). Bills made with GST off and returns of garments sold without tax are left out of the registers. The customer's phone is not exported. CGST is half the tax rounded up, as on the bill.
- **Code:** `apps/pos_windows/lib/data/gst_report_repository.dart`, `apps/pos_windows/lib/app/ui/gst_report_screen.dart`, `packages/core_domain/lib/src/csv.dart`.
- **Not built (the data is not captured yet):** sales by HSN code (needs a code on each style), sales to business customers with a customer GSTIN, purchases and input tax, a filing integration.
