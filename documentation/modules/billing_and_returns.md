# Module: billing and returns
Status: Return rules (P2-5a), saving returns and credit notes (P2-5b) and the returns screen with credit note slip (P2-5c) built 2026-11-21; nobody has seen the screens or printed a slip. Bill rules (P2-4a), saving a bill (P2-4b) and the register screen (P2-4c) built, 2026-11-21; nobody has seen the screen or printed a bill

Purpose: scanning, pricing, payment and returns at the counter.

- **Asked by owner:** fast barcode billing, returns and credit notes, optional customer details.
- **Rules:** SSOT 8-11, 15-16; ADR-0007, ADR-0011.
- **Pricing (built in `core_domain`, P1-3):** price = MRP, then markdown (percent or fixed amount), then cashier discount; a marked-down garment takes no cashier discount; a percentage rounds half up. The cashier's discount limit and the manager override are P1-4.
- **Owner decisions (2026-11-20):** payments are cash, UPI, card, split, store credit note (needs P2-5) and pay later (udhaar, separate task P2-4d, questions first). Bill number is `2026-27/C1/000123` (financial year April to March by India time, counter, that counter's running number). A scanned barcode that is already sold or not in stock is blocked with a clear message, no manager override. The bill shows on screen with an A4/PDF print dialog; thermal printing waits for the printer model.
- **Bill rules built (P2-4a):** `BillNumber`, `financialYearLabel`, `Payment`, `PaymentMode`, `settle` (change comes only out of cash; UPI or card above the total is refused). Assistant's choices, unconfirmed: serial runs 1 to 999999 per counter and year; counter numbers 1 to 99; 'card' and 'upi' are recorded as the cashier's confirmation only.
- **Saving a bill built (P2-4b, schema v4):** `SalesRepository` (`apps/pos_windows/lib/data/sales_repository.dart`): `lookup` (cashier-safe, no cost) and `saveBill` in one transaction (garments marked sold, bill, lines, payments, bill serial, audit, outbox). Tables `bills`, `bill_lines`, `bill_payments`, `bill_sequences`; the database refuses UPDATE and DELETE on the first three with triggers (E-AUDIT-1). Owner choices (2026-11-20): discount per garment only; optional customer name and phone typed on the bill (kept out of audit and outbox); a cashier discount over the limit is refused until the manager prompt exists (P2-4c). A markdown needs the `setMarkdown` permission (manager or owner). Assistant's choices, unconfirmed: phone 7 to 15 digits; name up to 80 characters; the counter number comes from setting `counter_no` (default 1); a returned garment can be sold again once P2-5 sets it back to in stock.
- **Register screen built (P2-4c, `apps/pos_windows/lib/app/ui/register_screen.dart`):** owner choices (2026-11-21): barcode box always focused (scanner types digits and Enter, or type by hand); bill lines left, big total and payment right; a discount over the cashier's limit is approved by a manager or owner typing their own password in a pop-up (the bill stays under the cashier, the approver's id goes in the audit details); after paying, the saved bill shows with Print bill (A4 PDF through the system print dialog) and New bill. Cashiers land on the register; manager and owner open it with a Billing button. Discounts are typed as `10%` or an amount (`parsePriceCut`). Not built: name search for tags that will not scan, markdown at the register (P2-4e), hold and resume, thermal receipt, a bill in progress is forgotten when the counter locks. The approval pop-up uses the normal sign-in check, so wrong tries count toward the lockout and an approval writes an `auth.signin` audit row.
- **Owner decisions for returns (2026-11-21):** 7 days to return (shop-calendar days, India time); inside it a cashier may take the return, after it a manager or owner types their password; refunds by cash, UPI, card, credit note or exchange (an exchange is a return paid out as a credit note applied to the new bill at once); credit notes last 180 days and the balance stays on the same note; a returned garment is put back in stock or marked damaged by the cashier (damaged needs a short note). **Return rules built (P2-5a):** `returnTiming`, `refundTotal`, `RefundMode`, `CreditNoteCode` (12 digits: 9900, counter, 5-digit serial, Luhn check; month 00 so it never reads as a garment barcode), `creditNoteLastDay`, `isCreditNoteValid`, `useCredit`, and `PaymentMode.creditNote`. Assistant's choices, unconfirmed: day counting by India calendar day (day 7 is inside, day 8 starts at India midnight); a note's last day is the issue day plus 180, valid to India midnight; credit note serials 1 to 99999 per counter; a restocked garment goes back to IN_STOCK (the RETURNED status in SSOT 7 stays unused unless you want it); each bill line can be returned once.
- **Returns saved (P2-5b, schema v5):** `ReturnsRepository` (`apps/pos_windows/lib/data/returns_repository.dart`): `lookup` and `saveReturn` in one transaction (garments back to IN_STOCK or DAMAGED, `returns`, `return_lines`, a credit note if that is the refund mode, audit, outbox). A bill line can be returned once (unique); a restocked garment can be sold and returned again. A return past the window needs `approveLateReturn` (new permission, manager and owner): either the person taking it has it, or an approver's role and id are passed and recorded. A damaged garment needs a note (kept out of audit and outbox). `SalesRepository.lookupCreditNote` and `saveBill(creditNotes: ...)`: a bill takes money off a note (not expired, enough balance, no change given back), recorded in `credit_note_uses`; balance is amount minus uses, so no row is ever edited. Tables `credit_notes`, `credit_note_uses`, `returns`, `return_lines` refuse UPDATE and DELETE (triggers); `bill_payments` now allows `credit_note` (table rebuilt in the migration). Cash, UPI and card refunds are only recorded as the mode; the money moves outside the app until the daybook (P2-6). Assistant's choices, unconfirmed: any cashier may take a return inside the window with no refund limit; a note is issued for the whole return total; credit note lookup is open to anyone who can sell. Not built: the screen, the credit note slip print, using a note at the register (P2-5c), exchange as one screen flow.
- **Returns screen built (P2-5c, `apps/pos_windows/lib/app/ui/returns_screen.dart`):** owner choices (2026-11-21): a Returns button for cashiers (top bar) and in Manager Mode; scan a garment, pick back in stock or damaged per garment (damaged needs a note), pick the pay-out (cash, UPI, card, credit note, exchange; none is chosen by default), Complete return; a late return opens the same manager-password pop-up as discounts (`approval_dialog.dart`). A credit note refund shows the code and valid-till and prints a one-page A4 slip (shop name, amount, Code 128C barcode, note number, issued, valid till; `credit_note_slip.dart`). Exchange saves the return as a credit note and opens the register with that note applied. At the register, scanning a credit note code in the barcode box applies it: it takes the smaller of its balance and what is still to pay, the rest stays on it; Exact cash covers what the notes do not. Not built: a reprint of an old credit note, a list of open credit notes, a refund limit, exchange of a different value as one step (the register simply takes the note's value off the new bill). Unverified: nobody has seen the screen or printed the slip; the slip barcode has not been scanned.
- **Shifts (P2-6b):** a bill or a return is saved only inside the signed-in person's open shift and writes daybook rows; see `cash_close_and_daybook.md`.
- **Workflow:** scan, price, optional customer, choose payment, save; print or show the bill. Returns scan the hangtag, check SOLD status, refund the exact net price, restock or mark damaged.
- **Tasks:** P1-3, P2-4a to P2-4e, P2-5a to P2-5c.
- **Evals first:** E-SALE-1, E-OFFLINE-1, E-RETURN-1, E-PRIV-1.
- **Not in scope:** promos, loyalty, card/UPI terminal integration, WhatsApp receipts, hold/resume (decide later).
- **Hardware (owner, 2026-11-17):** none yet. Receipts support both an ordinary A4 printer and a thermal receipt printer; a scanner is optional. Exact models are chosen later and then recorded here.
- **Open decisions:** printer and scanner models (owner will tell us).
